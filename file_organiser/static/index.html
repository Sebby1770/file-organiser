File Organiser

Scan a folder. We’ll say what to delete, what to review, and what to keep.

Local app · nothing uploaded · v4.1

Find space without guessing.

Scan your home folder, Downloads, or a folder you choose. File Organiser maps what it can read, then tells you what is safe to delete, what you should look at, and what to leave alone. Most hidden personal folders are skipped. Trash is a button you press — never an automatic wipe.

  1. 01Scan Home, Downloads, or any folder. Caches count.
  2. 02Advise Delete · Review · Keep, with a reason on every row.
  3. 03Trash Only the items you check. Protected paths stay put.
