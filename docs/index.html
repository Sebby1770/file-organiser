File Organiser is an app you run on your machine — not a command you type. It maps the disk, then splits every heavy folder into Delete, Review, and Keep. Caches and old installers go on Delete. Photos, documents, and keys stay on Keep. Nothing leaves this computer.

How it works

  1. 01Scan this computer

    Home, Downloads, Pictures, or any folder. Caches count. A sunburst shows where the bytes went.

  2. 02Read the advice

    Delete is regenerable junk. Review is large or stale. Keep is your life — photos, documents, keys, git.

  3. 03Trash what you check

    Only the Delete column can be sent to Trash in bulk. Review never auto-deletes. Keep is locked.

  4. 04Stay on this machine

    The app is a local window. No account. No upload. Quit and it stops.

What it does that a pretty ring doesn’t

Tells you what to delete

node_modules, __pycache__, browser caches, .DS_Store, empty files, old .dmg/.exe in Downloads — with a sentence of why.

Tells you what to keep

Pictures, Documents, Movies, Mail, Photos libraries, .ssh. The app will refuse to trash a home folder or a drive root.

Then does the work

Trash checked items, reclaim duplicate extras, organize Downloads into categories, reveal in Finder. Undo lives in the folder.

House rules

  1. Nothing is uploaded. The scan never leaves localhost.
  2. The app will not organize or trash $HOME or / as a whole.
  3. Bulk trash only accepts items already classified as Delete.
  4. Keys, mail, photo libraries, and version control are Keep — always.
  5. You confirm every trash action. There is no “clean my Mac” that fires itself.
