Frequently asked

Pricing without small-print surprises.

Is US$19 the final price?

It is the planned founding one-time price, not a live offer. The intended later individual price is US$29, but both may change before checkout; the actual live offer must show its update period and terms.

Why pay if the source is MIT licensed?

For signed delivery, simpler updates, history and reminder features, and a defined support target. The current MIT licence permits self-hosting and modification; the paid edition must compete on convenience and service.

Will free safety features be removed?

No removal is planned. Protected-path locks, fail-closed Trash, and understandable advice belong in the core product.

Is support guaranteed today?

No. Public-preview support has no response guarantee. The planned Supporter target is a first response within two business days after paid launch, not guaranteed resolution.

Can a business buy it?

Not yet. A higher business plan should launch only after signed delivery and dependable support exist. Use the early-access request to describe device count without sharing sensitive information.
